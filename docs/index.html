About

Alienizer replaces Latin characters with visually or phonetically similar glyphs from other writing systems. Depending on the extent of the substitution, the text will either retain its original silhouette while being encoded differently underneath, or it will increasingly look suspicious and strange, and eventually become unreadable. If you want to alienize your personal webexperience try one of the browserextensions above.
